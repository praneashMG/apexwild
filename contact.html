Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Most answers are here. Anything else — just send us a message above.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 24 hours of payment. Standard delivery is 3–5 business days; express options are available at checkout.

What is your return policy?

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Optics and blades must be returned unopened.

Can you help me choose the right bow setup?

Yes — and it's one of our most-requested services. Send us your height, wingspan, and intended use, or visit the store for a proper draw-length fitting.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship to Canada and the United States. Contact us for specialty international orders. Note: some optics and archery components are export-restricted.

Do your products comply with my state regulations?

Every product we list is verified against state and provincial wildlife guidelines. See our Safety & Regulations page for specific details by state.
