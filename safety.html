Weapon Handling

Firearm & Bow Safety Rules

Whether you carry a rifle, muzzleloader, or compound bow, four rules apply in every situation. No exceptions, no shortcuts.

  • 1 Treat every weapon as if it is loaded.
  • 2 Never point a muzzle at anything you do not intend to shoot.
  • 3 Keep your finger off the trigger until sights are on target.
  • 4 Be certain of your target, what is behind it, and what is beside it.

Archery-specific: inspect limbs, strings, and nocks before every draw. Never dry-fire a compound bow — the stored energy can destroy the riser and injure the shooter.

Regional Regulations at a Glance

Regulations vary by state and province. Always confirm your local wildlife agency's current handbook.

CO

Colorado

  • 500 sq. in. blaze orange (rifle)
  • Hunter safety card required (born after 1949)
  • Carcass tag must accompany meat
TX

Texas

  • 144 sq. in. blaze orange (rifle)
  • Online HIP certification for migratory birds
  • Annual public lands permit required
MT

Montana

  • 400 sq. in. blaze orange above waist
  • Non-resident combo tag draw required
  • Bear identification test for grizzly areas
WY

Wyoming

  • Blaze orange required in most rifle units
  • Preference point system for non-residents
  • Wolf tag separate from big-game license
PA

Pennsylvania

  • 250 sq. in. fluorescent orange minimum
  • Sunday hunting restrictions by WMU
  • Bear check station required same day
BC

British Columbia

  • Blaze orange in most GOS seasons
  • CORE hunter training mandatory
  • Compulsory species-identification quiz

Fair Chase

Ethics & Conservation

Hunting is conservation. Every ApexWild purchase supports the public lands and wildlife agencies that protect the resource.

Fair Chase Principles

No baiting where prohibited, no artificial light, no shooting from vehicles. Respect the animal and the chase.

Leave No Trace

Pack out everything you pack in. Minimize camp footprint and respect trail closures and seasonal wildlife corridors.

Support Wildlife Agencies

A portion of every sale goes to state wildlife conservation funds and public-access programs.

Hunter Safety Education

We promote and link to state-certified hunter ed courses for first-time hunters of any age.

Wildlife Management

Ethical harvest is a critical tool for herd health, disease control, and habitat balance.

Report Violations

Poaching harms every hunter. Report violations to your state's tip line — most accept anonymous reports.
