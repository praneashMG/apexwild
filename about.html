Our Mission

What Drives ApexWild

Three principles guide every product we stock and every interaction we have with customers.

Principle 01

Field-First Sourcing

We only stock gear that has survived real backcountry use and sub-zero conditions — no showroom-only products, no untested imports.

Principle 02

Honest Guidance

Every team member is an active hunter and tracker. We tell you what works and what doesn't in rugged terrain — even if it costs us a sale.

Principle 03

Conservation Minded

A portion of every sale goes directly to state wildlife protection funds and public-access wilderness programs that keep hunting traditions alive.

The Journey

Where It Began

A Cabin, Two Friends, and a Bad Set of Binoculars

In 2014, co-founders Mark Hensley and Ray Cordova were glassing a Montana ridge with binoculars that fogged up in the rain. That night, they started sketching what a hunting store built by actual hunters would look like.

A decade later, ApexWild serves 25,000+ hunters across North America — but the mission hasn't changed: only stock gear we'd trust in the field ourselves.
